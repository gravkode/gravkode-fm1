# LOCKS (0.35): parameter locks. EDIT tapped: LOCK mode, the white keys the steps on every page; a step held and a
# knob turned (OSC's, a drum's DRUM and SHAPE, the mixer's VOL and SEND): a lock on it, heard while it is held; a step
# off can hold locks (no note: its locks alone); EDIT held and a white key: that step's locks cleared. Playing, a
# step's locks hold until the track's next step; recording live in LOCK mode, the knobs lock the step playing. Saved
# with the pattern. tools/build.py terykons --host, then
#   terykons/build/host/terykons_host terykons/tests/locks.txt terykons/build/host
